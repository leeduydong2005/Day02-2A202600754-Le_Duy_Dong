# Research Notes

## Similar Patterns

| Tool / Pattern | Solves what? | Strength | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jira/Trello Labels | Mark task as Blocked | Simple and visible | Depends on people updating correctly |
| GitHub PR Review Status | Shows review delay | Good for engineering workflow | Does not show business/module impact |
| Daily Standup Form | Collects status and blockers | Structured input | Still needs manual synthesis |
| Slack/Teams Summary | Summarizes conversation | Good for chat recap | Does not connect chat to task dependency |
| Risk Register | Tracks project risks | Good for formal project management | Often updated too late or manually |

## Takeaway

The best first version should not be a full autonomous agent. It should be a workflow that combines structured updates, task/PR signals and AI summarization into a blocker risk table reviewed by the team leader.

## Non-AI Alternative

A non-AI solution could include:

- Strict daily blocker form.
- Mandatory "Blocked" label.
- Dashboard for tasks In Progress too long.
- PR review SLA.
- Manual risk register.

This improves discipline but still requires the team leader to connect scattered signals manually.
